# Experience VR — Rune Stones Model

## Status and authority

Status: **CURRENT / IMPLEMENTED**. This document owns the five natural Rune Stones and the special Ether Rune from tuning through physical handoff. Scenario owns their narrative order; Resonator and finale owners take over after the natural array is complete.

## Natural Rune domain

The natural family is `R / T / K / L / S` (Earth, Wood, Fire, Metal, Water). `RuneStoneProgressionController` owns tuned and installed family truth. The physical actor owns `FREE → LOCKED_BY_ASTRO → CARRIED_ORBIT → SOCKET_CAPTURE → INSTALLED`; release before capture returns the stone to `FREE` at its current position. The Attractor resolves eligibility from tuned-family truth, while installation readiness is consulted only for the platform handoff.

Inside the platform handoff volume, a ready natural Rune leaves carried orbit without passing through `FREE`. The installation interaction follows the live Binder anchors through approach, bridge opening and descent, then commits `INSTALLED`. The bridge actor owns only its physical/presentation state. Readiness, tuning and installed truth remain outside it. Reconstruction restores installed stones and bridges without replaying transient choreography.

Binder readiness normally follows completed portfolio sectors. Live `HIDDEN → ARRIVING` reveal transitions also feed the shared platform-energy presentation. The same current presentation route supplies the energetic lightning associated with keystone availability/installation choreography; the Rune domain does not require a historical `RUNE_INSTALL` profile name.

## Late natural progression and Ether boundary

After Tier 4 and the fourth natural Rune are both complete, Scenario enters the controlled final-Water attempt (`5.15`). The Water target reaches the intentional two-ring ceiling and cycles; the semantic rejection, not Rune state, advances Scenario to the Ether intervention (`5.20`).

Ether is special rather than a sixth natural family. Scenario unlocks its tuning at `5.30`; the tuned physical Ether stone is revealed at `5.40`, uses Rune scanning/locking and special carried transport, then hands off to `EtherMonkeyCaptureInteraction` instead of a natural socket. Capture leaves the same stone at the Monkey anchor and commits the Water-installation-readiness override. That override reveals the Water Binder and permits Water to use the ordinary natural Rune transport/install lifecycle at `5.50`.

The installed-family crossing from four to five emits `FIVE_ELEMENTAL_RUNES_INSTALLED` and establishes the full natural array at `5.60`. Ether remains outside `installedRuneFamilies`.

## Downstream handoff

Rune ownership ends at committed stone/Ether state and Binder readiness:

- Scenario owns controlled rejection, Ether communication and the point route through `100.10`;
- Resonator owners read installed sectors and own Metal/Water controls, field geometry, acquisition and `waterSyncLock`;
- Guidance owns the full-Resonator teaching and final-Water hint lifecycle;
- portfolio and finale actors own the final card, farewell, world release, credits, brand slate and XR exit.

Implemented Ether, Water and finale progression is therefore not Rune-domain future work. New finale behavior must not be added here.

## Ownership invariants

- Scenario capabilities can gate special story actions, but do not replace physical target, transport or installation laws.
- Presentation and audio observe Rune truth; they do not commit tuning or installation.
- Reconstruction hydrates settled truth and never replays capture, installation, lightning or communications.
- Water synchronization is not Rune truth. Installing Water makes the advanced Resonator possible; it does not itself solve the final puzzle.
