# Experience VR — Asterion Resonator Model

## Status and authority

Status: **CURRENT / IMPLEMENTED**. This is the primary Resonator model. Detailed descriptor math, containment and rendering belong to [VR_ASTERION_RESONATOR_FIELD_MODEL.md](VR_ASTERION_RESONATOR_FIELD_MODEL.md); the authored point graph belongs to the Scenario model.

## Runtime role

The Asterion Sphere is acquired through the Furnace path, equipped as a tool and used to acquire a platform sector. A legal target must be available, installed/powered and held by the Sphere for the acquisition interval before `SECTOR LOCK`. Lock grants local sector control; it does not grant Rune knowledge, portfolio completion or final pull eligibility.

Earth, Wood and Fire expose their implemented local sector motion. Metal and Water each expose two discrete `0..3` controls through the sector-control interaction. Metal changes lateral/depth field coverage and rounding. Water changes the field's frequency/color and intensity/halo presentation; Water does not select Glyph families or replace containment/acquisition truth.

The Resonator field is a read-only result of powered sectors and their controls. The field actor owns its descriptor and `waterSyncLock`; field presentation and lightning observe that truth. Target acquisition owns per-target rings and `PULL_READY`. Large Glyph actors own physical angular/radial motion and their dark-versus-ready materials.

## Late progression boundary

The current route is implemented:

```text
Tier 4 + fourth natural Rune
→ controlled final-Water two-ring rejection
→ Ether intervention, tuning and Monkey capture
→ Water installation
→ full Resonator acknowledgement
→ final Water hunt and portfolio completion
```

Before synchronization, final `haiku-cosmos` acquisition is capped at two rings and cycles at the ceiling. Composition maps that domain event to the Scenario rejection only in the late Water context. After Water installation and full-Resonator teaching, the active final puzzle uses all current controls.

The implemented balanced configuration is `222 / M22 / W22`: Earth, Wood and Fire at 2, both Metal controls at 2, and both Water controls at 2. The field actor derives `waterSyncLock` from that exact state. Lock enables the ordinary three-ring policy for final Water and the existing pull/acquisition path; it also drives the coherent field response and correctly tuned Resonator lightning.

There is no additional Water Sync Contact state, strong Haiku damping or final-Water acceleration. Those explored ideas were rejected and are not product backlog. Difficulty comes from the current slowly rotating, black, depth/scale-oscillating target and the need to acquire and track it.

## Downstream finale

At `5.70`, the final Water hunt completes Tier 5 through the normal crystal/card lifecycle. Scenario then orchestrates the final Monkey farewell, world release/whiteout, credits, Orange Monkey VR brand slate and `END_XR_SESSION`. Resonator owners do not own those presentations and do not infer their completion.
