# Names and Language

Status: **CURRENT**, synchronized with the implemented PL/EN product surfaces.

- The umbrella portfolio name and future editorial copy may still evolve; that does not make runtime localization unresolved.
- The entry shell owns the active `pl` / `en` selection, persists it, and propagates it through the document language into the selected presentation.
- Classic 2D, Experience 3D and portfolio/content records expose implemented PL/EN presentation through their bounded copy/content resolvers.
- Experience VR receives the selected locale from composition. Monkey communication, Player Guide, objectives, Furnace, finale, credits and portal/portfolio cards resolve their implemented PL/EN copy in their presentation owners; Scenario remains language-agnostic.
- `Orange Monkey VR` is the portfolio-facing identity in both languages. `Experience VR` remains the internal/runtime and technical-documentation name; these are two naming layers for the same third experience, not separate modes.
- `Classic 2D` and `Experience 3D` are the canonical current experience-category names. Existing localized entry labels remain owned by `src/main.js`; this document does not invent replacements.
- Canonical copy documents may preserve a source/reference language in their excerpts even when runtime data contains both locales. That editorial role must not be confused with missing runtime localization.
